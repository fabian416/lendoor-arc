Arc testnet · chain 5042002 October 9, 2026 14 transactions, all successful

What Lendoor is

Lendoor lends USDC with no collateral to consumers in Argentina and Peru, inside the Lemon wallet, on Celo. More than 4,500 loans since December 2025, with tickets from 1 to 75 USDC that climb one step with every on-time repayment.

Risk is decided in two layers: an admission step with identity, on-chain wallet signals and in-app behavior, and then the repayment history. Counting on-time payments predicts default far better than any admission score. What we brought to Arc is exactly that contract stack.

Why Arc

  • Native USDC as asset and as gas. One currency to deposit, lend, repay and pay for transactions. The borrower needs no other token.
  • Institutional capital in the currency of the loan. Thousands of consumer microloans in Peru and Argentina are a long-tail asset that does not reach investors today.
  • Built-in risk protection. Next is a first-loss tranche from the originator inside the vault, and the deficit recognized in the same transaction as the default.
  • Repayment in local currency. StableFX with wARS and wPEN, after the program.
